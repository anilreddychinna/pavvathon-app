OUR STORY

It Starts With a Paav. It Grows Into a Movement.

PAVVATHON takes inspiration from the Telugu word Paav (పావు) — a quarter. The idea is simple: meaningful journeys begin with a small step.

Our signature 11.11 KM distance gives people a welcoming challenge, while our wider movement brings together running, cycling, healthy living and community action.

We are not defined only by finish lines. We are defined by the people who show up, encourage one another and leave something good behind.
